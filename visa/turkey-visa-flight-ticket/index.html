How to apply, step by step

The order matters more than people expect. Do these out of sequence and you end up with a lapsed booking or an appointment you cannot fill.

  1. 1

    Check e-Visa eligibility on evisa.gov.tr

    That is the official site. Look-alike sites charge a markup for the identical thing.

  2. 2

    Check passport validity

    Turkey wants at least 150 days from entry for most e-Visa nationalities. Stricter than the usual six months, and regularly missed.

  3. 3

    Apply online

    Ten minutes. The visa lands by email, often within the hour.

  4. 4

    Print it

    Do not stake your holiday on airport wifi and a phone at five in the morning.

  5. 5

    Have onward travel ready

    Airline staff check this at check-in in your departure city, before Turkey gets any say in it.

  6. 6

    Carry accommodation details

    Immigration can and does ask where you are staying.

  7. 7

    Keep the e-Visa with the passport

    You will be asked for it again on the way out.

What the application actually requires

  • A return or onward flight booking: checked at check-in and on arrival.
  • Hotel booking or host address for the duration of the stay.
  • Passport valid at least 150 days from entry for most e-visa nationalities.
  • Proof of funds, occasionally requested at the border.
On unpaid reservations Turkish immigration authorities require evidence of accommodation and onward travel for visitors; airlines enforce this at check-in under carrier-liability rules.

Turkey: your questions

Do I need a return ticket for Turkey?

Yes in practice. Airlines check onward travel at check-in, and Turkish immigration may ask on arrival. A verifiable onward booking covers both.

Is a hotel booking needed for a Turkish e-visa?

The online form does not always demand one, but immigration can ask where you are staying. Carrying a confirmed booking removes the risk.

Worth knowing before you go

  • Istanbul has two airports far apart. Check whether you are flying into IST or SAW before booking a hotel.
  • The Istanbul Card works on trams, ferries, buses and the metro, and the Bosphorus ferry is the best value sightseeing anywhere.
  • Cappadocia balloons are cancelled often for wind. Give yourself three mornings there, not one.
  • Bargaining is expected in the Grand Bazaar and nowhere else. Fixed prices in ordinary shops are fixed.
