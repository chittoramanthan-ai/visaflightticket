What the application actually requires

  • A Schedule of Stay form covering every day from arrival to departure.
  • Flight itinerary with confirmed arrival and departure dates matching day 1 and the final day.
  • Hotel bookings whose check-in and check-out dates match the schedule, city by city.
  • Bank statements, usually for the last six months.
On unpaid reservations Japanese embassies and consulates require a schedule of stay and supporting travel documents; applications are typically lodged through an accredited travel agency.

Four ways this application goes wrong

None of these are exotic. All of them are avoidable in the ten minutes before you submit.

Schedule that does not match the bookings

The single most common Japan rejection. If the schedule says Kyoto on day 4, there must be a Kyoto booking for that night.

Missing internal moves

Tokyo to Osaka on day 5 should appear in the schedule, not just in your head.

Applying too early

Japanese visas are usually issued with a three-month entry window. Applying six months out wastes it.

Assuming you can apply direct

In many countries you must apply through an accredited agency, not at the consulate counter.

Japan visa: flight and hotel questions

Does Japan require a confirmed flight booking?

You must submit a flight itinerary with your dates. A verifiable reservation meets the requirement; Japanese missions do not require proof of payment.

How detailed must the schedule of stay be?

Day by day: the date, the city, the accommodation name and its phone number. Your hotel bookings should back up every line of it.

Can you match hotel bookings to a multi-city schedule?

Yes — give us the city order and nights in each, and we issue one booking per city so your schedule reconciles cleanly. That is the bundle.
