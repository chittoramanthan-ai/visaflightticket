How to apply, step by step

The order matters more than people expect. Do these out of sequence and you end up with a lapsed booking or an appointment you cannot fill.

  1. 1

    Check whether you need a visa at all

    Many nationalities enter free under the exemption scheme. Do not buy something you do not need.

  2. 2

    If you do need one, use the official e-Visa portal

    thaievisa.go.th. Agent sites charge a fee to type the same form for you.

  3. 3

    Sort proof of onward travel first

    This is where Thailand catches people, and it happens at the check-in desk in your home city, not in Bangkok.

  4. 4

    Check the onward date against your permitted stay

    A 45-day onward flight on a 30-day exemption defeats the point, and staff do notice.

  5. 5

    Have funds evidence to hand

    10,000 THB per person officially. Spot-checked rather than always checked.

  6. 6

    Fill in the arrival card

    It wants your accommodation address.

  7. 7

    Extend locally if you stay longer

    1,900 THB at an immigration office. Straightforward.

What the application actually requires

  • Proof of onward travel within your permitted stay. 30 days on the visa exemption Indians use, longer on a tourist visa.
  • Accommodation details for the arrival card and for immigration questions.
  • Proof of funds. Officially 10,000 THB per person, occasionally spot-checked.
  • For a tourist visa applied for in advance, the itinerary and accommodation in the application pack.
On unpaid reservations Thai immigration requires visitors to hold evidence of onward travel within the permitted period, and airlines enforce it at check-in because carriers are liable for returning refused passengers.

Four ways this application goes wrong

None of these are exotic. Every one of them is fixable in the ten minutes before you hit submit.

Flying in one-way on a visa exemption

Denied boarding is common on this route. It is an airline decision, not an immigration one.

Onward flight after the permitted stay ends

A 45-day onward booking on a 30-day exemption defeats the purpose.

Land-border onward bookings

Bus and train tickets to Malaysia or Laos are usually accepted, but airline staff read flight bookings faster.

Thailand: your questions

How long can Indians stay in Thailand without a visa?

30 days on the visa exemption. A tourist visa gets you longer, and a 30-day extension is available at an immigration office for THB 1,900. Thailand has changed this allowance more than once recently, so check the official portal before booking a long trip.

Do I really need proof of onward travel for Thailand?

Yes, and it is enforced at check-in in your departure city. Airlines are financially liable if you are refused entry, so they check before boarding.

Does a bus or train ticket count?

Usually, provided it crosses an international border within your permitted stay. A flight booking is what airline systems display most readily.

How long does the onward booking need to be valid?

Only until you have boarded and cleared immigration. That is why buying a full fare for this is poor value.

Worth knowing before you go

  • Indians enter visa free for 30 days. That allowance has moved more than once, so confirm it on the official portal before you book a longer trip. You still need proof of onward travel either way, and airlines check it in India.
  • Grab and Bolt work in Bangkok and are cheaper than metered taxis from the airport, which often refuse the meter.
  • The BTS and MRT do not accept notes at some machines. Keep coins or buy a Rabbit card.
  • Songkran in mid-April shuts the country for a week of water fights. Either plan for it or avoid it entirely.
